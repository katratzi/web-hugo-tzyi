A wee wonderful app for sorting through your photos, one moment at a time. Built for iPhone and iPad, working directly with your Apple Photos library. No account, no import needed.

{{< figure align=center src="/appstore/white.svg" link="https://apps.apple.com/PLACEHOLDER_MOMENTS_APP_URL" alt="Download on the App Store" >}}

We all take 20 versions of the same photo. A few portraits, another angle of the view, perhaps several attempts to get everyone looking at the camera. Sorting them afterwards can become a job for another day.

Moments breaks that job into little piles. Choose a day out, a holiday, or just an afternoon, then work through the photos at your own pace. Keep your favourites, archive the extras, or mark a few for deletion.

## How To Use

### 1) Create a Moment

Start by creating a new Moment and choosing a photo from the occasion you'd like to sort. Pick a rough length — Mini Moment, Grand Day Out, Adventure, or Epic Journey — and Moments will try to find the surrounding photos.

Check the suggested start and end photos, and adjust them if needed. You can also choose the range manually. Give your Moment a name, such as "Weekend in Edinburgh" or "Ella's Birthday", so it's easy to return to later.

### 2) Find Your Piles

Moments groups photos using visual similarity, time, and location. Similar shots sit together, making it easier to compare them without jumping around your library. Photos within each pile run from oldest to newest.

If the piles feel too broad or too small, open the Grouping controls. Adjust Visual Similarity, Time, and Location to make the matching stricter or broader. The piles update automatically, and your sorting choices stay attached to the photos.

Photos that don't match another pile appear in "Ungrouped photos", so you can review those too. Larger Moments are prepared in smaller batches, letting you work through a little at a time.

### 3) Sort Your Photos

Open a pile to start reviewing. Choose Keep, Delete, or Ignore, then tap photos in "Will Archive" to move them into your chosen group.

- **Keep** — the photos you want in your main Moment album.
- **Archive** — the extra shots you want to keep in a separate Archive album.
- **Delete** — photos you're marking for a separate deletion step later.
- **Ignore** — photos you want to leave out of the album sorting.

Changed your mind? Tap a photo in the top row to move it back to "Will Archive", or press and hold it to choose another action.

Once you've picked your keeps, tap the pile's progress circle to mark the remaining unsorted photos as Archive. Simply appearing in "Will Archive" doesn't change a photo in Apple Photos — it still needs an Archive decision before it will be synced.

### 4) Get a Little Help

Use **Suggest Keeps** if you'd like a starting point. Moments looks at image quality and, where available, faces to help choose photos to keep. It marks its picks as Keep; you can review and change those choices afterwards.

For a closer look, open the slideshow. Browse larger versions of the photos and make your sorting choices there too.

<!-- Screenshot: the slideshow displaying a portrait or scene, with its sorting controls visible. -->

### 5) Sync to Apple Photos

Choose **Create Albums**, or link your Moment to an existing album. Your keeps go into the main album, with the extras in a companion Archive album.

When you're ready, tap **Sync to Photos**. Only photos marked Keep or Archive are added to those albums. Ignored photos, delete candidates, and photos you haven't sorted are left out of that sync.

Archiving doesn't hide photos from your library or free up storage. It gives the extra shots their own album, while your main album holds the photos you want to revisit.

Deletion is separate. Marking a photo Delete doesn't remove it immediately, and syncing doesn't delete it either. Use **Delete Photos** and confirm when you're ready; those photos move to Recently Deleted in Apple Photos.

## A Few Handy Tips

**Take your time.** Your Moments and sorting choices are saved, so you can return and pick up where you left off.

**Missing photos?** If you've given Moments access to selected photos only, it can only work with those. Use Adjust Selected Photos to share more, or review the app's Photos permission in Settings. Photos stored in iCloud may also need time to download.

**Added more photos later?** Use Rescan Range to check for new photos within the Moment's date range.

**Try a smaller range.** A single afternoon or day out is a good place to begin while you get a feel for the piles and controls.
