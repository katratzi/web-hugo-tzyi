A little quality of life feature for this release (v8), rest timer notifications.  All local to the device, if you minimise the app you now get a little ios notification letting you know when you rest is done.  A few people had requested this in reviews, so thanks to everyone who took the time to leave a review and suggest it.  Enjoy the update, and good luck on your fitness journey!
